# Amazon Prime Video Content Analysis 📊🎬

📌 Project Overview
This project is a data analysis and visualization dashboard built using Power BI, focusing on the content available on Amazon Prime Video.
The goal of this project is to explore patterns in movies and TV shows, understand content distribution across genres, ratings, countries, and time, and transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

This project demonstrates my skills as a Data Analyst, including:

Data cleaning and transformation

Data modeling

Dashboard design

Insight-driven storytelling

🗂️ Dataset Description

The dataset represents Amazon Prime Video titles and includes information such as:

Show ID

Type (Movie / TV Show)

Title

Director & Cast

Country

Date Added

Release Year

Rating

Duration

Genre (Listed In)

Description

The dataset spans content released from 1920 to 2021, giving a long-term view of how Prime Video’s catalog has evolved.

🔧 Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI Desktop – Data visualization & dashboard creation

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

DAX – Measures and calculated fields

GitHub – Project hosting and documentation

🔄 Data Collection & Preparation Process

Data Collection

The dataset was sourced from publicly available Amazon Prime Video title listings.

It was initially provided in a structured tabular format.

Data Cleaning

Removed duplicates and handled missing values.

Standardized columns such as ratings, duration, and genres.

Split multi-genre fields for better aggregation.

Converted date fields into usable date formats.

Data Modeling

Created calculated measures such as:

Total titles

Total genres

Total ratings

Content distribution by year and type

Ensured relationships and fields were optimized for performance.

📈 Insights & Storytelling
🎬 Content Growth Over Time

The dashboard clearly shows that Amazon Prime Video’s content library experienced rapid growth after the year 2000, with a sharp spike post-2015.
This reflects the streaming industry boom, where platforms aggressively expanded their libraries to compete for global audiences.

Story:
Amazon Prime Video transitioned from a modest catalog to a content-heavy platform in a relatively short time, prioritizing scale and variety.

🎥 Movies vs TV Shows

Movies dominate the platform, making up roughly 80% of total content.

TV Shows account for a smaller but steadily growing portion.

Story:
Prime Video initially focused more on movies to quickly build volume, while TV shows appear to be a more recent strategic investment for long-term viewer engagement.

🌍 Global Content Distribution

The United States leads in content production.

Significant contributions also come from India, the UK, and Canada.

Story:
Amazon Prime Video is not limited to Hollywood—it has evolved into a global platform, investing heavily in regional and international content to reach diverse audiences.

🎭 Genre Trends

Drama is the most dominant genre.

Comedy, Suspense, Documentary, and Kids content also have strong representation.

Story:
The dominance of Drama suggests a focus on story-driven, emotionally engaging content, while the presence of kids and documentary genres highlights Amazon’s intent to cater to all age groups.

🔞 Ratings Distribution

Most content falls under 13+ and 16+ ratings.

Limited content is targeted strictly for children or adults-only audiences.

Story:
Prime Video’s catalog is largely designed for teen and family audiences, balancing maturity with accessibility.

📌 Key Takeaways

Amazon Prime Video has aggressively expanded its content library in recent years.

Movies remain the core content type, but TV shows are gaining traction.

The platform has a strong global presence with diverse regional content.

Drama and family-friendly ratings dominate the catalog, indicating broad audience targeting.
